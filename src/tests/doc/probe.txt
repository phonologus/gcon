The probe page
==============

Every construct of the documentation's Markdown, for the renderers'
golden files: emphasis, strong, code with -flags, a backslash * for a
literal star, a page, a heading, and a URL
(https://example.com/x?a=1&b=2). Escaped: <, &, "quotes", a backslash \
and a leading .dot that roff must not take as a request.
