comments.icn
============

comments: a probe of doc comments (src/tests/doc.sh), each of Go's
rules.

It has a file comment, at its top.

total
-----

    procedure total(L)

total adds up the numbers in L.

The numbers may be integers or reals:

    total([1, 2.5])   # 3.5

It fails for an empty list.

point
-----

    record point(x, y)

point is a place, with x and y.
